## Decision

Wave R is sufficient to stop repeating its exact four-family configuration, but it is not evidence that GDM mapping
or X-native researcher discovery is globally saturated. The 96 planned native-X calls covered 24 unresolved
candidates and produced 31 stable unique Post IDs: five were already known and 26 were new to the cumulative
frontier. Exact hydration recovered 24 source-bound texts. Two distinct records remained `tool_error` after one
append-only singleton retry each, so 28 hydration calls produced 24 exact texts.

The retrieval marginal is materially below Wave Q: Wave R produced `26/96 = 0.2708` new IDs per call versus Wave Q's
`33/50 = 0.66`. Its semantic result is still useful. Luna selected eight Wave-R pretraining observations across four
candidates even though the deterministic pretraining-lexical screen matched only one of 24 exact texts. The correct
conclusion is therefore configuration-local semantic saturation: do not repeat the same four query configurations,
but preserve AI-native semantic adjudication and test a different source topology.

`Top` becomes the default mode for both semantic families. It attributed 24 new IDs in 48 calls and supplied 18 new
IDs that Latest did not find. `Latest` remains a measured challenger rather than a default: it attributed eight new
IDs in 48 calls, but only two were exclusive versus Top. The next retrieval experiment is an
official-account/project/thread topology seed handshake. It must pass source-bound native-X and exact-hydration
checks before a larger topology wave. No other lab should be scaled from this result.

Candidate names, handles, Bios, Post or Reply text, URLs, observation IDs, credentials, and per-candidate decisions
remain in owner-private roots. This tracked document contains only root labels, aggregate counts, and SHA-256
bindings. Nothing in this run authorizes state transition, export, outreach, or promotion.

## Exact hydration and lexical diagnostic

The primary hydration lane executed 26 exact `x_thread_fetch` calls and produced 24 source-bound full texts. It
recorded two `tool_error` results, zero `not_found`, zero ambiguous bindings, zero failed leaves, and zero fallback
calls. One append-only singleton retry per unresolved record reproduced `tool_error`. The reconciled denominator is
therefore:

- exact unique-text coverage: `24/26 = 92.31%`;
- total hydration calls including retries: `26 + 1 + 1 = 28`;
- exact texts per total hydration call: `24/28 = 0.8571`;
- persistent unavailable records: two distinct IDs, not four records and not evidence of absence.

The same deterministic, case-insensitive lexical screen used in Wave Q checked the terms `pretrain`,
`pre-training`, `tokeniz`, `tokenizer`, `training data`, `data mixture`, `training recipe`, `curriculum`,
`deduplicat`, `dataset`, `corpus`, `objective`, and `filter`. It matched only **1/24** Wave-R exact texts, and that
match came from `dataset`. This lexical result is a query-family diagnostic, not a classifier or eligibility rule.
Luna subsequently selected eight of the 24 exact observations, so lexical filtering would have destroyed recall and
must not become an upstream gate.

## Next experiment and stop conditions

The exact four Wave-R configurations are closed for repetition. Their raw and semantic marginal is measured, neither
tool errors nor output-format failures obscure the result, and another identical call set would not test a new
hypothesis.

The next GDM-only experiment is a topology seed handshake:

1. Freeze the existing cohort, cumulative known-ID set, and source-neutral strategy descriptor.
2. Seed official-lab, named project, and technical-thread surfaces separately so topology remains measurable.
3. Require native-X stable Post IDs plus explicit author/subject relationship; generic web results do not pass.
4. Exact-hydrate every seed result and require correct ID-and-author binding before semantic review.
5. Expand a topology only after the seed produces at least one new source-bound exact observation; otherwise revise
   or retire that topology without a large wave.

Within that handshake, both Top semantic families are defaults. Latest is an incremental challenger whose exclusive
yield must be reported after Top deduplication. The experiment remains bounded by deadline, one-shot ledger, no
fallback, private raw evidence, and candidate-free reporting.

Do not scale to OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Meta, Thinking Machines Lab, or another lab from this artifact. First evaluate
the GDM topology challenger, preserve its raw evidence and marginal denominators, and decide whether the source-neutral
controller and temporal evidence reducer are ready for a separately reviewed multi-lab phase.
