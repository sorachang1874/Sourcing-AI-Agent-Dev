> Author evidence only. This is not an independent-review artifact, an X-source-bound field claim, a Stage 2 `GO`,
> permission for product integration, or authority to write CRM/outreach state. No real handle, Bio, or Post text is

## Decision

A single Grok answer and a fixed `25 candidates` / `200 observations` boundary materially understate the discoverable
population. Seven materially different native-X waves produced `29/12/6/20/25/5/1` new unique accounts. Wave 4 and
wave 5 rebounded after the first three waves appeared to decline, proving that a simple one-wave or monotonic-yield stop
would have terminated too early.

The final campaign contains `99` candidate rows and `98` case-insensitive unique handles. The last two strategies
added `5/104 = 0.048` and `1/70 = 0.014` new unique accounts per raw native-X call, after the current-team frontier and
a final coverage-audit strategy were both exercised. The exploratory operator paused collection to move capacity to
source hydration; it did not declare the population exhausted. The replay evaluator returns
`insufficient_proof / continue_expansion` because legacy prior-handle membership and query-family-to-call attribution
were not independently machine-bound. No candidate, observation, answer-length, or native-X-call target caused the
pause.

The business search had no candidate, observation, or per-wave call success ceiling. Every process retained an
emergency `64`-turn boundary, a 30-minute process-group deadline/kill boundary, one execution process, and no provider
fallback. Those are operational safety controls, not recall targets.

## What is proved, and the next gate

This campaign proves that adaptive Grok-native X exploration can build a much larger lead pool than a single response,
and that strategy diversity matters as much as within-wave persistence. It also proves that model-authored counts and
strict-output claims need local reconciliation.

It does not prove exhaustive OpenAI/pretraining coverage, replayable Post bodies, source-bound Bios/IDs, correct
researcher-role labels, or correctness of the `98` accounts. Exploratory collection is paused while capacity moves to
hydration, but the formal stop owner remains `insufficient_proof / continue_expansion`; another materially different
wave remains valid. Casefold merge, raw-session replay and target/prompt/request/model/source binding are now complete
for this author artifact. The remaining gates are:

1. hydrate stable account identity, exact Bio snapshot, canonical Post identity/time/author, and relationships;
2. run the AI-first professional-experience proxy reviewer only on explicitly labeled source-bound or
   model-mediated-unverified text;
3. measure evidence completeness, precision, conditional recall against a frozen gold view, unique yield/call,
   latency, cost, and human-review load before product promotion.
